What is the difference between a regular tag and an empty tag?

According to the textbook,An empty element usually has only one tag. Before the closing angled bracket of an empty element there will often be a space and a forward slash character. A Regular Tag has both an opening and a closing tag.

What does the term white space collapsing mean?

When the browser comes across two or more spaces next to each other, it only displays one space. Similarly if it comes across a line break, it treats that as a single space too. This is known as white space collapsing.
